Frequently Asked Questions

What is New Ras El Hekma City?

New Ras El Hekma City is an Emirati investment on Egyptian soil, aiming to establish a city under the name "Modon Ras El Hekma."

Who owns Ras El Hekma?

Ownership still belongs to the Egyptian state; however, the current investments and returns are directed to the investing entity on that land, which is the Emirati company Modon Holding.
